FAQ

How does image compression work?
Our tool uses advanced compression algorithms to reduce image file size while maintaining visual quality. Images are processed locally in your browser, so your photos never leave your device.
What image formats are supported?
We support JPG, PNG, and WebP formats. You can convert between these formats during compression.
Is my data secure?
Yes! All image processing happens locally in your browser. No images are uploaded to our servers, ensuring complete privacy and security.
Can I compress multiple images at once?
Currently, we support single image compression. Batch processing feature is coming soon.
How much can I reduce file size?
Compression ratio depends on the original image, but typical savings range from 30-70% while maintaining good quality.
Do I need to download any software?
No, our tool is entirely web-based. Just open it in your browser and start compressing.
